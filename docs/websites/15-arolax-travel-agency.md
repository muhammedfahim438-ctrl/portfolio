## 📌 What This Site Is About
A **travel agency portfolio/promotional website** for "Luke Adam," a fictional **trusted travel guider based in Switzerland** (working since 2003, claiming 3.2k+ guided tourists). Promotes tour guiding services, showcases destinations, sells travel packages, and offers adventure experiences (skydiving, mountain biking).
