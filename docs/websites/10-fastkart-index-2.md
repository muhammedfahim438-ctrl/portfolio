## 📌 What This Site Is About
**Fastkart** (Sweetshop demo) — an online **bakery & grocery e-commerce** template featuring cakes, bread, cookies, biscuits, bagels, croissants, cupcakes, sandwiches, pies, and general grocery items. This is the **"Sweetshop"** variant of the Fastkart grocery marketplace template.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript**
- **Bootstrap** (Pixelstrap's Fastkart is Bootstrap-based)
- **jQuery** (core)
- **Pixelstrap** (template author)

## 🎨 Animations Used
- **Swiper.js** — hero banner and product carousel
- **Owl Carousel** — category/logo/product sliders
- **AOS / WOW.js** — scroll-triggered reveal animations
- **Range slider / Countdown timer plugins** — deal-of-the-day expiry
- **Slick slider** (likely, alongside Swiper) — product/tab sections
- **Theme-config panel** (color / RTL / dark mode toggle with inline SVG)
- **Hover effects**, modal popups, sticky deal-of-day

## 🎨 Design Style
- **Sweet / bakery-flavored e-commerce** (warm, appetizing palette)
- **Modern, clean, card-based** product grids
- **Large product imagery**, rounded corners, soft shadows
- **Heavy reliance on high-quality food photography**
- **Sticky header, mega menu, off-canvas cart, quick-view modal**

## 🎨 Color Palette
- **Primary:** Red/Crimson accent on white background
- **Secondary:** Soft cream, beige, pastel pink (bakery imagery)
- **Neutrals:** White / off-white
- **Dark text** for contrast

## ✅ Summary
A **Bootstrap-based sweetshop/grocery template** with bakery-focused design. Features product-heavy grids, multiple sliders, sticky deal-of-day, countdown timers, and theme customization (Light/Dark, LTR/RTL). Built on Bootstrap 5 + jQuery + Swiper/Owl Carousel + AOS animations.
