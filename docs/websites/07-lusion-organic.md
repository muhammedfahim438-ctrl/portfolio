# Lusion - Organic Food Demo

**URL:** https://lusion.arrowtheme.com/demo-organic/
**Theme/Demo:** Lusion (Home Organic) by ArrowTheme / ThimPress

---

## 📌 What This Site Is About
A **demo of the "Home Organic" layout** of the **Lusion Multipurpose eCommerce WordPress Theme**. Fully-functional **organic food eCommerce storefront** selling fruits, vegetables, organic produce boxes, and daily products — with cart, wishlist, comparison, quick-view, multi-currency, and multi-language features.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **WordPress** (CMS)
- **WooCommerce** (e-commerce)
- **Lusion Theme** by **ArrowTheme / ThimPress**
- **WPML or Polylang** (multilingual: English / French / German)
- **WooCommerce Currency Switcher**
- **WPBakery / Visual Composer** (page builder)

## 🎨 Animations Used
- **GSAP** (typical for Lusion — scroll/entry animations)
- **ScrollTrigger** (reveal-on-scroll)
- **Lenis** (smooth scroll)
- **Swiper.js / Slick** (sliders)
- **WOW.js + Animate.css** (entrance animations)
- **AJAX Add-to-Cart** with toast notifications
- **Modal popups** (Login/Register, Quick View)
- **Recent purchase notification** popups (social proof)
- **Newsletter subscription** popup
- **Hover-reveal** product cards
