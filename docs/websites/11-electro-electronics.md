## 📌 What This Site Is About
**Electro** is a popular **electronics-focused eCommerce HTML template** sold on ThemeForest. Designed for online stores selling **smartphones, laptops, tablets, cameras, audio equipment, gaming gear, and consumer electronics**. One of the best-selling electronics templates on the marketplace.

- **Countdown timer** — flash sale deals
- **Hover effects** on product cards
- **Sticky header with scroll-triggered style change**
