## 📌 What This Site Is About
A demo/promotional landing page for the **Salient WordPress theme's "SaaS" template** — sold on ThemeForest. The page is styled as a **fictional CRM/automation platform** (also called "Salient") to showcase the theme's capabilities for building **modern SaaS marketing websites**.
