---

## 📌 What This Site Is About
**Fastkart** is a **multi-purpose e-commerce / grocery marketplace template** by Pixelstrap. This page (`index-8.html`) is the **Grocery Marketplace** demo variant — positioned as a **multi-vendor grocery delivery platform** (similar to Instacart, BigBasket, or FreshDirect) where customers browse food categories, add items to cart, select delivery zones, and checkout.
